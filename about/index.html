About Full Kit Marketing

The name means something.

A kit refers to everything an athlete wears in competition: not only the jersey, but the socks, the footwear, the sunglasses. Pros don’t take the field with a kit that’s missing pieces, and they wear a kit made specifically for them. Full Kit Marketing was created to provide growing businesses with everything they need to level up.

After more than 13 years as an in-house marketer for everything from pre-launch startups to Fortune 500 companies, Rachel Beck founded Full Kit Marketing in 2026. Her experience as a senior leader gave her an in-depth understanding of enterprise marketing, and she realized that many of those strategies and tactics could be leveraged by small businesses as well.

Full Kit is guided by the belief that marketing works best when it’s like a pro’s kit: tailored, authentic, and purposeful.

P.S. A kit can also mean a juvenile fox. Foxes are known for being clever, resourceful problem solvers with a side of sauciness — which describes Full Kit Marketing, too.
