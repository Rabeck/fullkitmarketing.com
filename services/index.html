Questions

Common questions

What does Full Kit Marketing do?

Full Kit is a marketing consultancy for growing businesses, specializing in getting businesses with little or no marketing infrastructure up and running with the systems and roadmap they need to scale.

How is Full Kit Marketing different from an agency?

Full Kit Marketing is a lean consultancy, led by Rachel Beck, the owner and principal. Services include both marketing strategy and execution, specifically tailored to your needs. Domain specialists are brought in as needed to provide in-depth services including design, paid search, and more. We build a strategy around your goals and resources, then pull in only what you actually need to grow.

Who does Full Kit work with?

We primarily work with growing service-based businesses — SaaS, hospitality, trades, professional services, consulting and more — typically with 2–200 employees and 0–1 in-house marketers.

How much does it cost?

Everyone's favorite answer: It depends! The consulting rate is $175/hour, and projects generally start at around $3,000.

What are Full Kit's core service areas?

We are a full-stack marketing consultancy, meaning we have expertise across multiple marketing domains. Specialties are marketing strategy & planning; messaging & positioning; content strategy & creation; web strategy and SEO; marketing ops; reporting & analytics; and events. Every strategic plan is built around your actual business needs, budget, and goals.
